Analytics and storage

Dropfall game library and Dropfall Arena · Updated 10 September 2026

Dropfall Game Studio is our public brand. For privacy questions, access requests or deletion requests, contact dropfallgamestudio@gmail.com. Our games are intended for a general audience, not specifically for children. This notice describes optional analytics and browser storage.

You can browse and play without allowing analytics. This notice explains the optional measurement used by the library, Dropfall Arena, Super Face Pop, Big Racers and moFighter. Each game runs separately and asks for its own choice, so allowing analytics in one does not opt you in elsewhere.

Your choice comes first

Google Analytics only loads if analytics is enabled for this site and you choose Allow analytics. Until then, this integration sends nothing to Google Analytics. We also leave it off when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

Open Privacy choices in the library or game to change your choice. Every product has a separate choice, saved on that website in your browser for up to 180 days. If browser storage is unavailable, the choice lasts only for the current page.

What is measured

With your permission, Google Analytics receives page visits, which game details you open, saves, play-link clicks, and Arena game readiness, match starts and match completions. A play-link click means someone chose to open a game; it does not prove that game loaded or a match began.

Events contain fixed game and mode labels, not your player name, profile, score, search text or room code. We send a cleaned page address without query strings or fragments, a fixed page title, and no referring-page address. Google also receives technical information such as browser/device details and connection IP information and generates session and engagement information. This is not anonymous measurement.

We use a dedicated Dropfall Google Analytics account. This integration does not use Pelican Drop Studios analytics destinations, advertising audiences or advertising IDs. Google signals, advertising personalization and cross-domain linking are disabled in our tag configuration. This analytics choice is not advertising consent.

Cookies and browser storage

  • Analytics choice: local storage keys starting with dropfall:analytics-consent: remember your choice separately for the library and Arena.
  • Optional analytics cookies: after opt-in, cookies whose names begin with a product-specific dropfall_ prefix help Google distinguish browsers and sessions. We configure them for the current host only, with a maximum 28-day lifetime and without extending that lifetime on each visit.
  • Game features: saved games, local profiles, preferences and local scores use separate browser storage to provide those features. Refusing analytics does not disable them.

Turning analytics off

Disabling analytics stops future optional measurement by this integration and removes its analytics cookies on the current host. It cannot recall requests already in flight or erase information already received by Google. To clear browser-only preferences and local game data as well, use your browser’s site-data controls; doing so may remove saved games and local profiles.

Advertising

The free game library and games may show advertising supplied by Google AdSense. Google’s own consent message manages advertising choices where privacy regulations require it. Advertising can use cookies or similar technologies according to the choice shown in that message. Dropfall Game Studio uses its own advertising account and does not share Pelican Drop Studios advertising audiences or identifiers.

Google and other services

Google processes analytics information on its infrastructure, which may be outside your country. See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites. Google also provides an Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Google Analytics user and event data is configured for a two-month retention period, without resetting that period on new activity. Most aggregate reports are unaffected by this setting.

Hosting, security, fonts and online multiplayer still need ordinary network requests for the site to work. Those services can receive connection information independently of this optional analytics integration. This notice covers analytics and browser storage, not a promise that the site makes no other network requests.
