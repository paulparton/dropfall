# Phase 19 — Themed, layered arenas

## User request

Add themed arenas and spheres. Design backwards from the final surviving arena,
then add the penultimate pieces and full starting arena. Add multiple elevations,
connecting ramps and launch pads. Classic remains deferred.

## Implementation contract

1. Author a permanent 16m combat core with a terrace, two accessible ramps and
   two launch pads. Add intermediate side galleries, then outer extensions.
2. Outer layer warns at 26s and falls at 30s; middle warns at 46s and falls at
   50s. Core remains indefinitely. Stable Arena retains all layers.
3. Each elevated deck has at least one flush physical ramp with the same collapse
   layer. Decorations and pads follow that dependency. No invisible surviving pad.
4. Launch pads use bounded velocity, height gating and a 1.4s fighter lockout.
5. Distinct forge, skyport and orbital palettes/settings; original Blender
   Forge/Turbine/Orbit sphere shells retain the same physical sphere and team IDs.
6. Preserve screen-relative controls, local modes, reset behavior and old records.
   Version new map IDs rather than comparing incompatible leaderboard runs.

## Verification

- UE 5.8.1 editor and Mac native builds.
- Blender round-trip and Unreal bounds/material import gates.
- Automated floor/ramp collision, 3 collapse stages, reset, pad flight/lifecycle,
  surviving-terrace traversal, AI route selection and cosmetic physics parity.
- Render all three themes and inspect HUD safe area, markers and collapse stages.
- Leave a ready preview for local playtesting; do not claim cooked release readiness.
